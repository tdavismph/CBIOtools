Where the default figures come from

  • Structure and ratios. Maternal-child defaults follow IGHA/KIKOP practice: 5 persons per household, ~100 households per community with one paid/stipended CHW/CHV each, Care Group Volunteers (CGVs, volunteer mother-to-mother educators) covering 8–12 enrolled women, ~10 CGVs per Care Group, and 1–2 Care Groups per paid promoter. CHWs/CHVs make eleven scheduled 60-minute home visits per mother from the second trimester to the child's second birthday. Cardiometabolic defaults follow an urban NGO–IGHA hypertension demonstration: 3.6 urban persons per household, 15 households per Care Group Volunteer, and seven CGVs per Promoter (one Care Group).
  • Unit costs. Drawn from four IGHA partner budgets for 2025–2026, past project proposals and an earlier IGHA CBIO+ budgeting tool. Default salaries and stipends are fully loaded where the source budget loaded them.
  • Setup vs. recurring. One-time costs (census, initial training, tablets, equipment, and any Community Birthing Center / Casa Materna construction) are placed in the first year; recurring costs (stipends, supplies, refreshers, monitoring) repeat each year and are pro-rated in the first year by the setup fraction you set.
  • In-kind. Government or partner salaries, clinical care, medicines, and platform licensing that a project uses without paying cash are shown as a memo and are not added into the cash total.

Planning-level approximations for design work. Verify local wages, ratios, and prices before submitting a budget. This tool produces a draft, not a quote.
