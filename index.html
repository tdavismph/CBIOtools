CBIO+ Tools

Planning tools for projects that use the Census-Based, Impact-Oriented approach with Care Groups (CBIO+).

CBIO+ Project Budget Calculator Drafts a budget for a maternal and child health project or a cardiometabolic (hypertension) project from the population covered, staffing ratios and the interventions selected. Shows the split between the implementing organization and IGHA, estimates impact, and downloads as an Excel workbook.
The calculator gives planning-level drafts. Verify local wages, ratios and prices before submitting a budget.
